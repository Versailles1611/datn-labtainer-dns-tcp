BAI LAB: MO PHONG TAN CONG DNS HIJACKING
==========================================

BOI CANH
--------
He thong gom 3 may ao tren cung mot mang LAN (172.20.0.0/24):

  - victim     (172.20.0.2): may nan nhan, can phan giai ten portal.lab.local.
  - dns-server (172.20.0.3): may chu DNS hop le, hien tai zone lab.local,
                          bao gom:
                              portal.lab.local  A  172.20.0.3
  - attacker   (172.20.0.4): may cua ke tan cong (ban).

TINH TRANG BAN DAU
------------------
- Trong may victim, /etc/resolv.conf dang tro toi may chu DNS hop le:
      nameserver 172.20.0.3
- Trong may attacker da co san BIND9 duoc cai san voi vung zone lab.local,
  nhung CHUA cau hinh tra ve IP gia mao.

NHIEM VU CUA BAN (muc do TRUNG BINH)
-------------------------------------
1. Tren attacker, sua file zone /etc/bind/db.lab.local de tra ve IP gia mao
   172.20.0.99 cho portal.lab.local.

      sudo nano /etc/bind/db.lab.local

2. Khoi dong / nap lai BIND9 tren attacker de zone moi co hieu luc:

      sudo service bind9 start
      sudo service bind9 reload   #neu bind dang chay

3. Tren victim, chuyen huong DNS sang may chu gia mao bang cach sua file
   /etc/resolv.conf:

      sudo nano /etc/resolv.conf
      # doi thanh: nameserver 172.20.0.4

4. Kiem chung: tren victim, truy van lai ten mien:

      dig portal.lab.local +short

   ket qua dung khi tra ve 172.20.0.99 (chu khong phai 172.20.0.3 nua).

KIEM TRA KET QUA
----------------
Tu Linux host, chay lenh:

    checkwork

De xem danh sach cac checkwork: kiem tra cau hinh resolver cua nan nhan,
ket qua phan giai ten, log truy van tren rogue server, v.v.

KET THUC
--------
Khi hoan thanh, chay:

    stoplab

Luu y: ket qua checkwork sau khi stoplab se duoc luu de cham diem.
