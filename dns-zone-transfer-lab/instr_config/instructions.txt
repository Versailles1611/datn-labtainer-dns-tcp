BAI LAB: DNS ZONE TRANSFER (AXFR)
==========================================

BOI CANH
--------
He thong gom 3 may ao tren mang LAN 172.21.0.0/24:

  - victim     (172.21.0.2): may nhan vien, dung DNS server 172.21.0.3.
  - dns-server (172.21.0.3): BIND9 phuc vu zone example.com.
  - attacker   (172.21.0.4): may cua ban, co dig va tcpdump.

MUC TIEU
--------
Kiem tra xem DNS server co cho phep zone transfer (AXFR) tu bat ky may nao
khong. Neu co, ke tan cong lay duoc toan bo ban ghi DNS noi bo.

BUOC 1 - Thu zone transfer
--------------------------
Tren attacker:

    dig @172.21.0.3 example.com AXFR

Hoac luu ket qua:

    dig @172.21.0.3 example.com AXFR > /tmp/zone.txt
    cat /tmp/zone.txt

Neu thanh cong, ket qua co dang:

    example.com.        300 IN SOA ns.example.com. admin.example.com. (...)
    example.com.        300 IN NS  ns.example.com.
    ns.example.com.     300 IN A   172.21.0.3
    www.example.com.    300 IN A   172.21.0.3
    ...
    ;; XFR size: 6 records

BUOC 2 - Doc cac ban ghi quan trong
-----------------------------------
Tu ket qua zone transfer, xac dinh:
  - SOA record (Start of Authority)
  - NS record (name server)
  - A record cua cac may noi bo

BUOC 3 - Xem cau hinh sai tren server
-------------------------------------
Tren dns-server:

    cat /etc/bind/named.conf.local

Chu y dong:

    allow-transfer { any; };

Day la cau hinh nguy hiem. Cach sua:

    allow-transfer { 127.0.0.1; };
    // hoac
    allow-transfer { none; };

Sau khi sua, khoi dong lai bind9:

    sudo systemctl restart bind9

BUOC 4 - Thu lai
----------------
Tren attacker:

    dig @172.21.0.3 example.com AXFR

Phai bi tu choi (Transfer failed).

KIEM TRA KET QUA
----------------
Tu Linux host:

    checkwork

4 checkwork:
    cw_zone_transfer - server tra loi zone transfer
    cw_soa_record    - lay duoc SOA record
    cw_ns_record     - lay duoc NS record
    cw_host_records  - liet ke duoc cac A record noi bo
