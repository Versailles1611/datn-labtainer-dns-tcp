BAI LAB: MO PHONG TAN CONG TCP HIJACKING
==========================================

BOI CANH
--------
He thong gom 3 may ao tren cung mot mang LAN (172.21.0.0/24):

  - victim   (172.21.0.2): may nan nhan, mo phien TCP toi server.
  - server   (172.21.0.3): dich vu TCP cong 9999, ghi moi du lieu nhan
                        duoc vao /var/log/session.log.
  - attacker (172.21.0.4): may cua ke tan cong (ban), co san scapy,
                        tcpdump va 2 script ho tro: arp-poison, hijack.py.

NHIEM VU CUA BAN (muc do TRUNG BINH)
-------------------------------------
Buoc 1. Tren attacker, ARP spoofing de chen giua victim va server:

      cd ~/.local/bin
      sudo setsid nohup ./arp-poison eth0 >/tmp/arp.log 2>&1 < /dev/null &
      disown

  Kiem tra tien trinh con song: ps aux | grep arp-poison
  Kiem tra tren victim: ip neigh | grep 172.21.0.3 phai thay dia chi
  172.21.0.3 tro toi MAC cua attacker (02:42:ac:15:00:04).
  Chu y: dung chung 1 terminal, khong dong cua so, khong pkill,
  khong restart lab giua chung.

Buoc 2. Tren attacker, bat capture TRUOC khi mo phien:

      sudo tcpdump -Z root -U -n -w ~/session.pcap 'tcp port 9999'

  De cua so nay chay (co -U de ghi file ngay lap tuc).

Buoc 3. Tren victim, mo phien TCP toi server:

      start-session

  Kiem tra tren server: sudo cat /var/log/session.log phai thay HELLO.
  (Server don luong: chi mo 1 phien, khong mo them ket noi khac.)

Buoc 4. Tren attacker, dung capture (Ctrl+C) roi doc SEQ/ACK:

      sudo tcpdump -nn -r ~/session.pcap

  Tim goi tin victim -> server co du lieu HELLO (cong dich 9999), ghi lai:
    - so sequence TIEP THEO cua victim (seq + do dai payload), vi du goi
      HELLO\n dai 6 byte hien "seq 1:7" thi next-seq = so tuyet doi cua 7,
    - so acknowledgment hien tai (so tuyet doi cuoi cua server),
    - cong nguon (sport) cua victim.
  Vi du tron ven: SYN victim "seq 2452713148", du lieu "seq 1:7" nghia la
  next-seq = 2452713148 + 1 + 6 = 2452713155; server "seq 4262417" va du
  lieu "seq 1:11" nghia la ack = 4262417 + 1 + 10 = 4262428; sport 48844.

Buoc 5. Tren attacker, chen goi tin gia mao vao phien:

      sudo python3 hijack.py <seq> <ack> <sport>

  Vi du: sudo python3 hijack.py 2452713155 4262428 48844
  (Nho cd ~/.local/bin truoc vi sudo khong thay PATH cua ubuntu.)

  Kiem tra tren server: sudo cat /var/log/session.log phai thay HIJACKED.

KIEM TRA KET QUA
----------------
Tu Linux host, chay lenh:

    checkwork

De xem 4 checkwork: phien TCP (tcp_session_ok), ARP poison (tcp_arp_ok),
pcap sniff (tcp_sniffed_ok), goi tin gia mao (tcp_hijacked_ok).

KET THUC
--------
Khi hoan thanh, chay:

    stoplab

Luu y: ket qua checkwork sau khi stoplab se duoc luu de cham diem.
